LingoPlay design directions

Five static mockups of the same two screens (lobby and a vocabulary round). Nothing is clickable except the Lobby / Game switch in the bottom-right corner of each page.

A · ClubhouseOld-school game site in the Playok tradition: light, dense, plain tables, system fonts. B · PaperNewspaper and dictionary: cream paper, serif type, rules instead of boxes, one red accent. C · ChunkyLight and playful: flat colour, rounded type, press-down buttons, room cards. D · Street signHand-painted shop sign: condensed capitals, red, yellow and teal, tickets and a chalkboard. E · TerminalDark and flat, one accent colour, monospace numbers, keyboard hints. No gradients or glow.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so B to E need an internet connection to look right.
